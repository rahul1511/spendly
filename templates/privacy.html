Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses and protects your information

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense details you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and notes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to operate Spendly: to show your expenses, generate summaries and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and retained for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses third-party services such as Google Fonts to load typefaces. These services may receive technical information like your IP address when you load a page. We do not share your expense data with them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
